FOMOBOT — OVERNIGHT REVIEW (Sep 24)
===================================

THE BOTTOM LINE
---------------
I analysed two months of your bot's data (Aug 29 - Sep 24). The honest result:
the bot does not have a money-making edge yet. Every version lost money in testing,
including v15.1 and v16.2. This is common: most trading bots never find one.
Fees are the biggest single reason the losses are as large as they are.

What I built tonight doesn't hide that. It makes the bot tell you the truth in every
alert, protects its data from glitches, and lets us test new ideas in days instead of
weeks. If a real edge exists, it can now be found and proven quickly. If it doesn't,
the bot keeps you from paying for that lesson with real money.


THE NUMBERS
-----------
Paper trades (the bot's own simulated trades, with fees):
  389 trades | 13% winners | -$76.63 total | about -3.9% per trade after fees
  Before fees about -1.4% per trade, so fees (~2.6% per buy+sell) more than double the loss.
  Every version lost:  v15.0 -$45.03 | v15.1 -$6.61 | v16.0 -$11.50 | v16.1 -$12.10

Your recorded real trades (/bought and /sell):
  59 trades | 22 winners | -$69.72 on $720 traded (-9.7%)
  Biggest losses: SHARK -$24.98 and USWS -$7.99 (both rugged to ~zero), catonfone -$11.27
  Best: CATE +$10.59, JEANCOIN +$6.84, STACY +$5.02

Every coin the bot looked at (24,874 of them, one per coin per 30 minutes):
  The typical coin was flat 30, 60 and 120 minutes later. No setting of score, market cap,
  liquidity, momentum, buy/sell ratio or volume beat fees in both halves of the month.
  The bot's extra filter stages did not pick better coins than its first-pass list.
  A machine-learning model could spot which coins would move a lot, but not which
  direction. Its top picks hit +50% 43% of the time but -15% 79% of the time.

Where the paper losses came from:
  224 trades closed by the 30-minute time stop at about -1.4% (then fees on top)
   92 trades hit the stop-loss, and lost 15% on average although the stop is ~8%
      (prices were checked too slowly, so coins fell past the stop)
   37 take-profits won +29% on average: the bot does catch runners,
      just not often enough to pay for everything else.


WHAT CHANGED TONIGHT (all tested: 272 automated tests pass)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Every BUY alert now shows its track record, for example:
   "TRACK RECORD (ENTRY OPTION, paper, last 14d): 107 tests | 19% wins | avg -5.3% per
    trade after fees. This alert type has LOST money in testing..."
   /trackrecord shows every alert type.
2. New /signalpolicy proven: only alert you for alert types whose record is positive AND
   consistent (the same statistical test that guards real money). Today nothing passes,
   so you would get no BUY alerts until something earns it.
3. Price glitch protection. The data had fake moves like +383,398% in one hour (a price-feed
   error). These can no longer count as wins, trigger fake take-profits, or unlock
   real-money trading.
4. Faster paper exits: checked every 6 seconds instead of once per slow scan, so paper
   results match what a live bot would really get.
5. Price-path recorder: every coin the bot considers is now followed for 2 hours, even after
   it drops off the trending lists, with the exact second it first hit +10/20/30/50/100%
   or -5/8/10/15/25/50%. That's around 1,000 coins a day of clean data, enough to test any
   take-profit/stop rule exactly in days instead of waiting weeks for paper trades.
6. One-click research: double-click research_report.command to rerun this whole analysis
   yourself in about 15 seconds.
7. Rent reclaim (/reclaim): each coin bought from the bot wallet locks ~0.002 SOL in a token
   account. The bot now gets it back after selling. This only applies to the live bot wallet,
   not your Phantom/Fomo wallet.

WHAT I RECOMMEND
----------------
1. Install the new version (instructions in the chat). Keep your .env and never delete
   ~/Library/Application Support/FomoBot/fomo_master.db.
2. Send /signalpolicy proven in Telegram. The alerts you've been trading have lost money in
   testing; this stops them until one type proves itself. Prefer to keep seeing them? Then
   at least read the TRACK RECORD line before buying.
3. Don't put in new real money for now. Keep real autopilot OFF and don't set /bankroll.
4. Check your trading app's fee. The paper results assume ~2.6% per buy+sell including
   slippage. If your app charges ~1% per swap, fees alone cost you ~2% per round trip, and
   that's before price moves.
5. Leave the bot running about a week, then run export_review.command and send me the file.
   With ~7,000 recorded price paths I can test exit rules exactly and check whether any
   corner of the market has a real edge. If one exists, we build around it. If not, you'll
   know for sure without having lost more money finding out.

No bot can promise profit. What this version does is tell you the truth quickly.
