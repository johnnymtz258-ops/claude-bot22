DATA / MODEL CHECK
------------------
The Sep-9 export is a fresh holdout of v13.3 logic (no v14 run marker). Its 81 completed delivered 30m paths showed weak reward/risk asymmetry. A later replay of v14's static capital box on this fresh dataset also failed to generalize well enough to authorize live entries. v15 therefore does not use that static box as its primary positive model.

The export's observations table had already pruned most older multi-day snapshots. Because v15's new path gate depends on sequences of observations, it cannot be honestly reconstructed for all 81 historical alerts. The build intentionally does NOT claim a historical v15 win rate. Instead, always-on current-build shadow simulation and a fresh proof gate collect forward evidence net of configured friction.
