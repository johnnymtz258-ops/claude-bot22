FOMOBOT WHALE COPY — QUICK START
================================

INSTALL / UPDATE
1. Stop the old bot (Control + C in its window).
2. Unzip and double-click start_mac.command in the new folder. Your .env is already inside;
   whales and history are kept (they live in ~/Library/Application Support/FomoBot).
3. Keep the Mac awake: lid open + charger in. If it sleeps you'll get a 💤 message saying what you missed.

WHAT HAPPENS BY ITSELF
  📌 A live card is pinned in Telegram and refreshes every minute: is it running, what got blocked and
     why, your positions with the plan, and the paper balance. Tap its buttons instead of typing.
  🔍 Every whale is profiled: 🟢 holder · 🔵 swing · 🟠 flipper · 🤖 bot. Flippers (sell within minutes)
     and losing whales are tracked but never alerted — you'd be buying their exit.
  🔎 Every 2h the scanner looks for new holders in today's runners and follows the ones whose trades
     made money for a copier buying a minute late (you get a message, with Unfollow).
  🤖 The paper autopilot trades $50 of a pretend $1,000 on every alert with the plan below.

TRACK A COIN YOU HOLD
  /add COIN  (or /add COIN at 850k with your entry) — or paste it in the dashboard's My trades tab.
  You get told at 2x / 3x / 5x / 10x, if it gives back 35% after 1.5x, at -40%, and when whales sell it.
  /coins lists them · /drop COIN stops one.

GETTING OUT OF A TRADE
  Dashboard → My trades / Autopilot: Sell 50% / Sell all (from the trading wallet) · Close on paper trades.
  Telegram: /sellnow COIN 50% · /sellall · the 🔴 Sell now button on live buys.
  Coins in your Fomo wallet are still sold in Fomo — the bot can only sell from the trading wallet.

LIVE AUTOPILOT (real money, optional — off by default)
  1. New wallet just for this, funded with only what you can lose.
  2. Add its private key to .env:  TRADING_PRIVATE_KEY=...   (never share .env or the folder after this!)
  3. Restart, then /live on. It starts in DRY RUN (nothing is sent). /live dry off to trade for real.
  0.05 SOL a trade (more on strong signals, less on weak ones or after losses, never over 1% of the pool),
  max 3 open, stops for the day at -0.3 SOL. /live off stops buying.
  Before every real buy: no token traps, and selling straight back must not lose more than 12% (honeypot check).
  Swaps go privately through Jito (no sandwich bots). 3 failed transactions in a row halt new buys for 30 minutes.

RUN IT 24/7 FOR FREE (recommended — the Mac sleeping cost money in your data)
  Follow SERVER_SETUP.txt: a free Oracle Cloud server, then double-click send_to_server.command.
  Dashboard from the server: open_server_dashboard.command · its log: server_log.command.

START BY ITSELF (on the Mac)
  Double-click install_autostart.command once (remove_autostart.command undoes it).

FREE RPC (Helius ran out?)
  FREE_RPC.txt: paste a free Chainstack link into SOLANA_RPC_HTTP= in .env and restart. 3 minutes.

HEALTH
  /health — live stream, each RPC provider, how fast whale trades arrive, live-trading safety state, crashes.

ONLY WHAT'S PROVEN (v29)
  The autopilot no longer buys every hype alert (that lost money). /edge shows which setups made money after costs
  on the last 7 days; paper tests one only when it won on both halves of the week, real money only after paper
  confirms it. Nothing passing = it stands aside, and hype alerts arrive silently, marked. EDGE_GATE 0 = old behavior.

MARKET PULSE
  /market (or the Market tab) — is the market hot or cold, why, what's trending, what to look out for. A brief every
  morning at 9 (MARKET_BRIEF_HOUR) and a note when it turns. In a cold market the live autopilot waits (MARKET_GUARD).

SELF-TUNING
  The hype filter measures what each of its rules blocked and loosens or tightens itself every 6h (you get a
  🧠 note in Telegram for each change). /tuning shows the numbers · /set HYPE_AUTOTUNE 0 stops the changes.

EVERY ALERT HAS THE SAME PLAN
  Sell half at 2x. Sell the rest if it falls 35% from its top, or 40% below your entry.
  Use the 🎯 Ping me at 2x button. The bot also nudges you at 2x / after a 35% drop / at -40%.

USEFUL COMMANDS (optional)
  /edge   /market   /positions   /whales   /paper   /live   /coins   /stats   /analyze WALLET   /find COIN   /export
  /set NAME VALUE changes a setting (/settings lists them), e.g. /set PAPER_TRADE_USD 25

TO SEND ME DATA FOR TUNING
  Type /export in Telegram — the bot sends you FomoBot_review.zip (no keys or tokens inside).
