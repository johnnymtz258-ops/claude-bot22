FOMOBOT WHALE COPY — QUICK START
================================

INSTALL / UPDATE
1. Stop the old bot (Control + C in its window).
2. Put this folder next to the old one. Copy your .env into it (Finder: Command+Shift+. shows
   hidden files) — or skip this and the launcher copies your Telegram settings itself.
3. Add these lines to .env if they aren't there:
     HELIUS_API_KEY=...        free at helius.dev (much faster alerts)
     MY_WALLETS=...            your PUBLIC wallet address (exact profit/loss)
4. Double-click start_mac.command.
5. Open http://localhost:8787 — or send /help to your bot.

Your tracked whales from the old bot come across automatically.

WHAT YOU'LL GET
  🟢 WHALE BUY (grade A/B/C)   a whale you follow bought — entry market cap, their real record
  🐋🐋 2ND WHALE IN            another of your whales bought the same coin
  🔴 WHALE EXITED / 🟠 SOLD x%  a whale is selling a coin YOU hold
  📈 COMMUNITY RUNNER          no whale, but a big spread-out crowd buying (measured separately)
  🚨 LIQUIDITY PULLED          only for real rugs (confirmed twice)
  No more "sell, it dropped" alarms. The exit signal is the whale leaving.

DAILY USE
  Reply to an alert:   /bought 20            or   /bought 20 at 850k
                       /sold 5  /sold 30%  /sold all
  (Not needed if MY_WALLETS is set — your trades are read from the chain automatically.)
  /positions   your coins, profit/loss, and which whales are still in
  /whales      which whales are actually worth copying (measured, not guessed)
  /hot         coins with 2+ of your whales

FIND NEW WHALES
  /find COIN              early buyers of a coin that already ran (e.g. CASHED)
  /find COIN1 COIN2       wallets early in BOTH are listed first
  /analyze WALLET         is this wallet copyable? then tap ➕ Follow
  /suggest                finds whales in today's biggest runners (more whales = more alerts)
  /scout now              whale picks: profitable wallets from today's runners, sent to you with Follow buttons

SELLING BETTER
  /exits                  which exit style works best on your whales + your too-early/too-late habits
  You'll get one nudge at 2x/3x/5x/10x, and a 🛡 warning if a 2x+ coin gives back 35% from its peak.
  /watch COIN 2x          ping me when it hits a market-cap target (/watches, /unwatch N)

BROWSER EXTENSION (optional)
  Chrome → chrome://extensions → Developer mode ON → Load unpacked → pick browser_extension.
  DexScreener / GMGN / Axiom / Photon / pump.fun pages then show which of your whales are in.

The old bot is untouched in the legacy folder.
