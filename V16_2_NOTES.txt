FOMOBOT v16.2 QUALITY MEASUREMENT
================================

Purpose
-------
Surgical accounting/reporting hotfix. Entry thresholds, discovery logic, safety checks,
and live sizing gates are unchanged from v16.1.

Changes
-------
1. FAST ENTRY now participates in proof_shadow_rows(), matching proof_candidate_eligibility().
2. v16.1 and v16.2 paper/proof outcomes share one quality cohort so installing this hotfix
   does not reset proof evidence or hide existing shadow results.
3. /paperreport now reports v16.2 and includes per-tier trade count, win rate, net P/L,
   ROI and friction for FAST/FLOW/STRONG/ENTRY OPTION/REVERSAL.
4. /edge and paper risk guard use the same v16.1+v16.2 quality cohort.
5. The tier report uses paper_positions.realized_pnl, the actual schema field.
6. Open paper positions and the shared external database remain untouched by the package.

Safety
------
REAL autopilot behavior is unchanged. This release does not loosen entry gates and does not
turn on live trading.
