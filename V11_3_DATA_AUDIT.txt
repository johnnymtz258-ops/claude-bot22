KEY FINDING #1 — RECENT CRASH/REBOUND MEMORY WAS MISSING
For the 12 actionable alerts covered by the continuous raw observation window, v11.2 only judged stability from the current/recent price shape. It could forget that a token had suffered a severe 5m crash shortly before the alert once the newest live snapshot bounced.

Examples:
- inuzard had a ~-13.5% 5m flush only ~83 seconds before the entry; it then fell ~-45% by 60m.
- BAILEY had roughly a -40.6% 5m flush about 10 minutes before its FLOW ENTRY; it later fell ~-86%.
- the later OTC entry had a ~-16% prior 5m flush while its 1h tape was still about -14.5%; it hit -10 before +15.
- moonkey had a ~-14% prior 5m flush and 1h tape still about -11.5%; it hit -10 about two minutes after the STRONG alert before later recovering.
- Grokstreet also had a prior ~-14% flush, but by the alert its 1h tape had repaired to about +8.9% and it delivered +15 before -10. This is why v11.3 does NOT simply block every token that crashed earlier.

v11.3 targeted rule:
- <= -20% 5m flush anywhere in prior 10m: wait.
- <= -12% 5m flush in prior 3m: wait.
- <= -12% 5m flush in prior 10m AND current 1h still below -5%: wait.

On the 12 raw-covered actionables, this rule would have held back inuzard, BAILEY, the later OTC and moonkey while preserving the clean OTC/PINK/Grokstreet examples. Two of the blocked setups later recovered, but both first suffered fee-relevant >=10% drawdowns. The gate can allow them later once the broader tape repairs; it is a delay, not a permanent blacklist.

Run-window GUARDIAN state-change ledger:

- EXIT_REVIEW: 11
- PROFITABLE: 10
- CONFIRMED: 3
- TAKE_PARTIAL: 2

The log emitted about 35 GUARDIAN messages. ANTSEM alone generated many internal ENTRY <-> WEAKENING/EXIT transitions because a small bounce around the -10% line repeatedly changed the state.

ANTSEM is the best example:
- recorded at $15 around 06:34 UTC
- ~06:51: v11.2 called EXIT REVIEW around -16.3% because one snapshot combined the loss with weak structure
- it subsequently recovered and eventually reached about +15.2%
- GUARDIAN correctly issued TAKE PARTIAL near that +15% window
- no partial sale was recorded; the position later rolled over and ultimately was sold around -19.7%

This shows both sides:
1) the fee-aware RECOVERY WATCH concept was valid; one soft structural snapshot should not necessarily trigger a tiny-position exit;
2) once +15% is reached, taking/recording a partial remains important because meme-coin profit windows can disappear.

v11.3 fix:
- SMALL structure-only EXIT above the hard line must persist ~45s.
- MID structure-only EXIT must persist ~30s.
- hard price-line or >=30% liquidity failure remains immediate.
- confirmed EXIT escalation bypasses the soft warning cooldown.
- WEAKENING recovery now has hysteresis so a tiny bounce does not instantly reset to ENTRY.
- repeated WEAKENING messages require materially worse return/drawdown/liquidity before re-alerting.

KEY FINDING #3 — DO NOT BROADLY LOWER LIQUIDITY / MICRO RULES
The missed-opportunity ledger found 67 run-window examples, including many impressive moves. But this ledger is deliberately survivor-selected: it records strong-flow states that later ran, not every similar state that failed.

There were 54 Solana missed-runner records; 34 began below the normal $50k liquidity floor. Broad tests of permissive low-liquidity conditions continued to produce too many -20% to -99% outcomes. Therefore v11.3 keeps:
- normal entry liquidity floor at $50k,
- strict MICRO rescue,
- safety/stability checks,
- Solana-only actionable chain default.

Several large ledger examples on BSC/Base/Robinhood are intentionally watch-only because those chains are not configured as actionable by default.

FLOW ENTRY NOTE
Only one FLOW ENTRY occurred in this run (BAILEY), and it failed badly after a prior crash/rebound. A broader market-only retrospective of permissive FLOW-like snapshots also had poor downside characteristics. v11.3 therefore does NOT loosen FLOW ENTRY. The recent-crash memory now applies to FLOW ENTRY as well.

ADAPTIVE CONFIDENCE NOTE
In this single-day 18-alert sample, neither the legacy score nor the displayed adaptive confidence showed a reliable monotonic relationship with 60-minute outcome. That sample is too small to rebuild the calibration model around, but it is enough to avoid treating the number as mature. v11.3 labels the calibration LEARNING until both endpoint and path cohorts are sufficiently populated. During LEARNING, /size is reduction-only: it can suggest less than the configured base, but not more than 1.00x.
