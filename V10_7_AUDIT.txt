KEY FINDING #1 — v10.6 WAS SAFER, BUT TOO MANY ENTRIES WERE ECONOMICALLY QUIET
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Six of the eight actionable alerts had 5-minute dollar volume equal to only about 0.024%-0.041% of market cap. They generally went nowhere. BITCAT and cat, the two meaningful winners, were around 0.276% and 1.047% respectively.

v10.7 change:
- Normal ENTRY OPTION requires >=0.08% 5m dollar turnover / market cap.
- STRONG ENTRY requires >=0.15%.

On this exact eight-alert sample, that filter would have removed the six low-motion alerts while retaining BITCAT and cat. That result is encouraging but is still a small sample and is not a profit guarantee.

KEY FINDING #2 — ONE IMPORTANT LOW-LIQUIDITY MOVE WAS MISSED
------------------------------------------------------------
TOGETHER was put on SCOUT at roughly 21:52:46 UTC around $0.0001127. It did not get a normal entry because liquidity was only about $28k-$29k, below the $50k normal floor, and its recent raw tape had been volatile. Around 21:53:56 it had recovered to about $0.0001192 with roughly:
- $29.3k liquidity / $117.3k market cap
- ~25% liquidity-to-MC
- ~11.2% of market cap traded in 5 minutes
- ~3.28x buys/sells
- ~291 swaps/5m
- 5m +1.2%, 1h -2.7%

From that recovered snapshot the observed move later reached roughly +68% without first hitting -10%.

v10.7 change:
A separate MICRO ENTRY path can rescue this type of setup WITHOUT lowering the normal $50k liquidity floor. It requires:
- $25k-$50k liquidity
- $70k-$400k market cap
- 18%-80% liquidity/MC
- >=5% 5m dollar turnover/MC
- >=1.8x buy/sell
- >=50 swaps/5m
- controlled 5m/1h move and scout distance
- strong score/confirmation
- RugCheck + holder verification
- a shorter 3-minute microcap stability window with recovery proof

An offline replay of the recent SCOUT set using these strict conditions selected TOGETHER and did not broadly open the liquidity gate to the weaker missed movers.

KEY FINDING #3 — PARTIAL PROFIT TRACKING EXISTED, BUT THE TELEGRAM WORKFLOW WAS AWKWARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
v10.6 already had internal partial-position math and /sellpct /sellusd commands. The problem was usability: TAKE PARTIAL PROFIT messages did not carry the contract and /sellpct required TICKER + percent, so replying directly to the advice did not work.

v10.7 change:
- TAKE PARTIAL PROFIT, DE-RISK MORE and EXIT/REDUCE alerts include the exact contract.
- After actually selling in Fomo, reply directly to the alert with:
    /sellpct 50
    /sellusd 2
  or friendly aliases:
    /sold 50%
    /sold $2
- The bot journals realized P/L on that leg, updates remaining_fraction, and keeps monitoring the remainder.
- /sell still records a full exit.
- These commands journal manual Fomo actions; they do not place the manual order.

TAKE-PROFIT LEVELS
------------------
The current +15% first checkpoint remains sensible for the reviewed sample: BITCAT, cat and the best missed microcap move all crossed +15%, while most low-motion alerts never came close. The partial-sale workflow is therefore improved rather than raising TP1 and risking giving fast meme-coin moves back. TP2 remains +35%, with the trailing-risk logic still active after TP1.

PACKAGING / MAC FIX
-------------------
All .command files in v10.7 are marked executable before packaging, including export_data.command, to avoid the access-privileges problem seen in v10.6.

BOTTOM LINE
-----------
v10.7 is intentionally not "more alerts at any cost." It does two things simultaneously:
1. suppresses economically quiet false-positive entry alerts; and
2. adds a narrow, high-flow, safety-verified path for exceptional low-liquidity microcaps that the fixed $50k floor could miss.

No filter can guarantee profitability. Meme coins can gap, rug, or reverse between scans. Continue using small position sizing and record actual buys/sells so the position-aware risk system has accurate information.
