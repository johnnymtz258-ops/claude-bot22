PURPOSE
-------
v16 shifts development emphasis from adding more rejection filters to better discovery and ranking.
It adds a public-on-chain Solana smart-wallet copy engine while retaining the existing broad
meme scanner, social/news sources, shadow engine, Guardian, and hardened optional execution.
GET READY scout chatter is OFF by default; internal scouting continues.

COPY ENGINE
-----------
- Up to 20 public Solana wallets by default; one standard logsSubscribe per wallet on one WSS connection.
- Helius RPC/WSS is used when HELIUS_API_KEY is configured; public Solana RPC/WSS is the fallback.
- Confirmed transaction fetch + final tracked-wallet balance-delta parsing.
- Failed transactions are ignored.
- Multi-hop swaps resolve to the final non-base token when SOL/WSOL or USDC is the base leg.
- Venue context recognizes Jupiter, Pump.fun/PumpSwap, Raydium, Meteora, generic swaps, and Jito hints.
- Partial leader sells become proportional follower paper exits.
- Duplicate WSS notifications are DB-idempotent on wallet/signature/action/token.
- Birdeye smart/top-flow data can discover wallet candidates; risky dev/bundler/sniper/insider-tagged
  candidates are not auto-promoted.
- /copyanalyze reconstructs recent confirmed SOL/USDC round trips for an approximate observed-history score.
- Paper copy is automatic and fee-aware at follower-time market price.

OPTIONAL LIVE COPY SAFETY
-------------------------
- OFF by default and starts with COPY kill switch ON.
- Reuses hardened live_execution.py; no second transaction signer/executor was created.
- New buys require the copy paper gate, live executor readiness, event freshness, liquidity/chase limits,
  wallet score, max positions, and daily-loss limit.
- Default gate: >=48h paper age, >=20 closed copy-paper trades, positive net paper P/L.
- Per logical follower order uses durable execution idempotency through the hardened executor.
- Follower sells verify actual token balance and mirror leader sell fraction.
- Exits remain permitted when the new-buy kill switch is ON.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
--------------------
Copying a historically profitable public wallet does not guarantee follower profit. Latency, slippage,
leader sizing, hidden hedges, token liquidity, wallet behavior changes, and incomplete reconstructed history
can all make follower results materially different. Paper-copy results should be reviewed before enabling live copy.
