FOMO BOT v14.0 CAPITAL FIRST — RELEASE NOTES
============================================

1. CAPITAL-FIRST ECONOMIC EDGE
- Added a final fee-aware public-market quality gate after the legacy classifier.
- Requires market depth, real dollar turnover, controlled momentum, sufficient prints and buyer confirmation.
- Existing security, stability, tape, commitment, chase and slippage gates remain in force.

2. UNIVERSAL BUY PERSISTENCE
- Normal/STRONG/FLOW/REVERSAL BUY candidates must survive ~75 seconds of fresh confirmation.
- Rejects a candidate that weakens >~2.5%, loses >~3% liquidity, or runs >~3% before confirmation.

3. TOKEN TRAUMA MEMORY
- A same-contract bot alert that experienced <=-15% 30m drawdown blocks another bot-guided entry for 24h by default.

4. CORE SELF-QUARANTINE
- New CAPITAL CORE governor starts PROBATION.
- Probation bot-guided size cap: $5.
- After >=6 completed 30m delivered paths, weak median upside / +10 hit rate / drawdown quality quarantines core BUY alerts.
- Quarantined candidates continue as CAPITAL_SHADOW and can re-prove the lane without live-risk alerts.

5. WEAK LANES WATCH-ONLY BY DEFAULT
- STRUCTURE BUY alerts OFF by default.
- MANUAL CHAIN BUY alerts OFF by default.
- MICRO/FRESH BUY alerts OFF by default.
- Discovery, diagnostics and learning remain active.

6. CAPITAL LOSS CAP
- Guardian hard price risk cannot be widened beyond ~8% by an old .env or old DB setting.
- Fees are no longer treated as a reason to tolerate 16-20% losses on small positions.

7. PROFIT REALIZATION
- Normal checkpoints ~+12% / +25%.
- Fee-aware TP1 can rise when needed for tiny positions.
- <=$25 positions at TP1 favor full realization.
- High-water giveback from a meaningful winner escalates to EXIT_REVIEW / full exit guidance.

8. TELEGRAM DELIVERY TRUTH
- sendMessage retries three times.
- Failed BUY delivery releases the action reservation and records ENTRY_DELIVERY_FAIL; no EARLY signal / long cooldown is consumed.
- Guardian TP, runner, exit and liquidity-warning flags advance only after confirmed delivery and remain retryable otherwise.
- /status reports Telegram delivery health.

9. UPGRADE-SAFE SIZE LIMITS
- old $20/$30 DB guidance is capped to $10 normal / $15 exceptional.
- optional REAL autopilot remains OFF unless explicitly armed and is capped to $5 per trade.
- small auto positions use full TP1 realization.

10. DIAGNOSTICS
- /status shows capital-core state, ENTRY_EDGE, ENTRY_DELIVERY_FAIL and CAPITAL_SHADOW counts.
- /why CONTRACT diagnoses capital edge and persistence without creating a pending BUY.

This build uses public/on-chain data only. It is decision-support software, not a guarantee of profit.
