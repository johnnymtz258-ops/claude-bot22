    How the autopilot trades

    • Takes every setup DayBot alerts you on — pretend money, nothing touches your accounts.
    • Buys at the next minute's price plus costs (in-play breakouts: only when the price breaks the first 5-minute high), risks 1% of its balance, never more cash than it has.
    • Sells by each setup's plan: in-play breakouts hold to 3:55pm unless stopped; SPY/QQQ band trades get out when a half-hour close falls back; end-of-day bounces sell at 3:59pm; the others sell half at the first target, move the stop to breakeven and sell the rest at the second.
    • Judge it after 20+ trades — a few trades are mostly luck.

    How the Invest lab works

    • Slow, well-studied ETF strategies, tested on all the free daily history Alpaca has (about 10 years, including the 2018 drop, the 2020 crash and the 2022 bear market), dividends included.
    • Each day's decision uses only that day's close and is traded at the next open, paying a cost on every dollar traded. The numbers are the textbook ones (200-day average, 12-month momentum…) — nothing was tuned on this data.
    • Most plans earn a bit less than holding SPY in a rising market. They exist to sidestep the big drops. If none beat holding, the honest plan is "keep buying a broad index fund" — and that's what it says.
    • It messages you only when your plan changes, and once a month. It never places a trade; you do it in the app, with fractional shares and no commission.

    How the crypto plan works

    • Trend-following from Catching Crypto Trends (Zarattini, Pagani & Barbon, 2025): nine trend models per coin, looking back 5 to 360 days. The score (0–9) is how many say "trending up".
    • Each coin gets an equal slice of your crypto money; it holds more of a coin the stronger and calmer its trend, and sells when the trend breaks. No trends = all cash — that's how it sidestepped most of 2022's crash.
    • It re-plans once a day when the daily candle closes and messages you only when something changes. It lags Bitcoin in big bull runs; it's built to keep you out of the crashes.
    • Costs: Robinhood charges about 1% each way through its spread — included in every number here.
