FOMO BOT v10.4 DATA-TUNED

This build adds the re-entry changes requested after reviewing the actual overnight database.

WHY:
The uploaded database showed 24 re-entry opportunities, and that family was materially weaker
than normal entry alerts, including several catastrophic collapses. The fix is NOT to increase
frequency or blindly raise the score. It changes the re-entry state machine.

CHANGES:
- Re-entry now starts as RE-ENTRY WATCH, not an immediate actionable entry.
- A setup must survive a 3-minute confirmation window and pass the entire structure again.
- Requires at least 12 minutes of observed history.
- Requires a real 10-minute base with <=18% range.
- Requires current 5m momentum between 0% and +5%.
- Requires B/S >=1.35x and >=20 swaps/5m.
- Requires sustained buyer pressure over multiple recent snapshots.
- Requires >=$60k liquidity and >=85% retention versus recent baseline.
- Rejects rebounds >35% from the recent low (helps reject near-zero wick/dead-cat artifacts).
- Rejects a recent >=45% crash inside the re-entry window.
- Requires BOTH RugCheck and holder-distribution verification.
- Existing v10.3 hard rug/holder/liquidity protections remain.
- Real autopilot re-entry is OFF by default even if live autopilot is armed.
- /check revalidates the stricter re-entry structure and safety.

The goal is fewer but materially cleaner second-chance opportunities.
