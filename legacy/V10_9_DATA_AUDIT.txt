WHY MICRO ENTRY WAS NOT BROADLY LOOSENED
A raw backtest of permissive $25k–$50k liquidity "strong flow" states found that most also suffered severe downside. In one tested candidate set, 6 of 7 hit at least -15% within an hour even though several also printed +25% or more first. Examples included tokens that later fell roughly -64% to -99%. Therefore v10.9 does not simply lower the liquidity floor. It adds earlier FLOW scouts, verified normal-liquidity FLOW ENTRY, and a controlled second-leg reset while retaining strict safety/stability checks.

CHANGES IMPLEMENTED FROM THIS AUDIT
1. Remove hard manual journal position-count/daily-loss/size/chase recording locks.
2. Preserve exact Telegram alert/check snapshot for /bought replies.
3. Allow exact-contract manual recording without a prior bot signal.
4. Add exact price and market-cap overrides.
5. Replace rigid 15m manual time-stop with horizon-aware monitoring.
6. Add live hold horizon classifications and /hold /plan commands.
7. Tighten 1h falling-knife floor to -20%.
8. Tighten STRONG dollar-flow requirements.
9. Add high-flow internal scout and FLOW ENTRY path without weakening safety.
10. Add controlled >=60m second-leg re-alert when fresh verified flow returns and no open position remains.
11. Keep MICRO rescue strict due adverse downside/rug backtest.
12. Keep +15% and +35% profit checkpoints; recent data repeatedly showed profitable windows that later reversed sharply.

This audit is descriptive/backtested on the available history. It does not guarantee future returns or protection from rugs/liquidity loss.
