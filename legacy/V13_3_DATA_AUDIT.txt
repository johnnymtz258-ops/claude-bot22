WHY v13.3 EXISTS
----------------
v13.2 fixed the Quality Governor itself, but the fresh post-v13.2 holdout exposed two independent control-flow failures that can make a stricter bot feel worse even when its classifier is unchanged:
1. an ENTRY signal created by an older build could suppress a newly valid setup for the full legacy eight-hour cooldown;
2. the recent-crash memory window was short enough for a violent flush to expire before a superficial rebound became actionable.

- that snapshot had no ordinary near-entry blocker

v13.2 still looked up the legacy EARLY signal over eight hours. Because those old alerts were from v13.0, not the current model, the fresh setup could be suppressed before the current-build stability/tape/confirmation pipeline got a fair evaluation.

v13.3 fix:
- same-build ENTRY_SENT rows keep the full 8h cooldown, including across restarts;
- older-build alerts get a 60m upgrade grace by default;
- after that grace, the current build can evaluate a fresh scout independently when no recorded position is open;
- the ordinary same-build second-leg rules remain intact.

ZCAT VIOLENT-FLUSH MEMORY
-------------------------
At ZCAT's v13.2 entry:
- live entry snapshot: 5m +3.63%, 1h -2.39%, B/S 1.83x, 17 swaps, ~1.03% turnover/MC
- within the preceding 30 minutes, raw observations recorded a -17.99% five-minute print about 10.8 minutes before entry
- the old recent-crash lookback was only 10 minutes, so that flush had just expired from the hard crash-memory gate
- the following 30m path reached -20.17% and finished -8.52%

v13.3 fix:
- preserve at least 30 minutes of crash memory;
- a <= -17.5% five-minute raw observation inside that window blocks the recovery entry;
- legacy .env values of 10m / -20% cannot silently weaken the new default after upgrade.

Counterfactual replay on the real exported observation history:
- ZCAT is blocked by the new crash-memory rule.
- v13.2 Jimothy remains allowed by the stability rule.
- HeeHaw and Leafy old-build cooldowns are cleared after the 60m cross-build grace; they still must pass all current live gates before any alert.

WHAT WAS NOT LOOSENED
---------------------
The audit did NOT support a broad relaxation just to increase alert count:
- v13.0 cross-chain BUY cohorts remained weak; strict v13.1 cross-chain shapes stay in place.
- isolated low-liquidity runners existed, but neighboring low-liquidity candidates still showed severe drawdown risk; the normal $50K Solana liquidity floor remains.
- isolated >+10% 1h continuation runners existed, but historical continuation cohorts showed substantial severe-drawdown risk; the normal +10% 1h ceiling is not broadly raised.
- the core entry classifier, tape-preservation gate, commitment gate and lane thresholds are otherwise unchanged from v13.2.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
This is not a promise of profit. The fresh holdout is small and meme-coin returns are highly non-stationary. v13.3 is designed to remove a demonstrated stale-version suppression bug, remember a demonstrated dangerous flush longer, and make future alert droughts measurable before any further model retuning.
