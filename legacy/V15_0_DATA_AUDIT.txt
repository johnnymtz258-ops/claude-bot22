INTERPRETATION
--------------
The old model did find runners, but the downside frequency was too close to the upside-hit frequency for a small, fee-sensitive bankroll. A STRONG label did not imply a strong endpoint. The multi-day data also varied materially by day, indicating regime dependence rather than one stable fixed threshold set.

V14 STATIC CAPITAL BOX — FRESH GENERALIZATION CHECK
----------------------------------------------------
The v14 normal Solana static economic gate was replayed against the v13.3 delivered-entry snapshots in this later Sep-9 dataset. It selected 15 paths.

Those 15 paths had:
- median maximum return: +10.97%
- median minimum return: -6.93%
- median endpoint: -3.23%
- reached +10%: 53.3%
- reached -8% or worse: 46.7%
- reached -15% or worse: 13.3%

Conclusion: the v14 fixed liquidity/turnover/momentum box did not generalize well enough to be the positive authorization model. It has therefore been demoted in v15. v15 uses a broad veto layer plus stateful path confirmation, current-market breadth, historical downside analogs, execution-quality preflight and forward proof.

WHY v15 DOES NOT CLAIM A HISTORICAL WIN RATE
---------------------------------------------
The Decision Ledger and outcomes span the multi-day holdout, but the observations table in this export begins only around Sep 9 05:19 local-system time and ends around Sep 9 17:41. Older observation snapshots had been pruned by maintenance. The new v15 setup-path gate requires consecutive observations across time, so it cannot be honestly replayed for every one of the 81 old alerts.

It would be misleading to synthesize missing snapshots or call a partial retrospective pass a backtest. Therefore v15 intentionally starts with an always-on shadow/proof phase and grades only fresh v15 forward trades net of configured friction.

DESIGN CONSEQUENCES
-------------------
1. No attempt to maximize alert count.
2. Solana-first actionable lane.
3. One-frame score/classifier cannot authorize a trade by itself.
4. Broad market RISK_OFF can veto new exposure.
5. Historical analog layer is downside-veto only; it cannot create a BUY.
6. Real route/friction is measured before action when Jupiter is available.
7. Jupiter Tokens V2 suspicious/banned status is an independent veto when configured.
8. Every fully qualified setup can create a shadow trade before live authorization.
9. Fresh-build proof must become ACTIVE before live BUY guidance.
10. Explicit /bankroll is required before non-zero live-money sizing.

LIMITATION
----------
No historical audit, classifier, proof gate or API can guarantee positive future returns. Meme-token distributions are non-stationary; liquidity, routing, fees and adversarial behavior can change faster than the model.
