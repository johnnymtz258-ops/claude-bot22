FOMO BOT v10.9 NOLOCK+HORIZON

1. Manual ledger locks removed
- /buy and /bought no longer reject a journal entry due to manual open-position count, daily realized P/L, manual amount, or alert-to-Telegram price drift.
- Existing positions can be averaged with another manual buy.
- Manual positions no longer consume the optional REAL autopilot position cap.
- Automated execution keeps its own AUTO_LIVE safeguards.

2. Exact Telegram trade snapshots
- Entry/check/re-entry messages are saved in telegram_trade_context with price, market cap and liquidity.
- Reply /bought 5 after the Fomo purchase to use that message snapshot.
- /buy CONTRACT AMOUNT works without a prior bot signal.
- Explicit overrides: price or mc.

3. Hold/time-horizon engine
- Every actionable entry shows a TRADE HORIZON.
- /hold and /plan re-evaluate a live token/position.
- Classifications: SCALP, SHORT-TERM, SHORT SWING, EXTENDED HOLD CANDIDATE, or RISK/EXIT REVIEW.
- The old rigid 15-minute manual time-stop was replaced by horizon-aware monitoring. A no-progress exit requires actual weak live structure rather than time alone.

4. Entry-quality changes from Aug-28 data
- 1h falling-knife floor tightened to -20%.
- STRONG requires >=25 swaps and >=0.25% 5m dollar turnover/MC.
- High-flow internal scouts added.
- FLOW ENTRY added for verified normal-liquidity high-flow setups; not permitted for REAL autopilot.
- Controlled second-leg alert reset after >=60m when strong fresh flow returns and the prior position is no longer open.
- MICRO thresholds intentionally remain strict because broad low-liquidity rescue backtests showed severe downside/rug frequency.

5. v10.8 state-ledger fix retained
- Closed historical positions cannot be resurrected from stale old folders.
