FOMOBOT v16.2 QUALITY MEASUREMENT — START HERE
===============================================

UPGRADE WITHOUT LOSING HISTORY
1) Stop the older bot with Control+C.
2) Unzip this release to a NEW folder.
3) DO NOT delete ~/Library/Application Support/FomoBot/fomo_master.db.
4) Keep your existing .env / API configuration. The release contains only .env.example.
5) Double-click start_mac.command.
6) In Telegram send: /status, /edge, /paperreport.

WHAT v16.2 FIXES
- FAST ENTRY now counts in the proof gate when it is already marked proof-eligible.
- v16.1 + v16.2 paper/proof outcomes stay in one quality cohort because entry gates did not change.
- /paperreport now shows per-tier count, win rate, net P/L, ROI and friction.
- /edge and the paper risk circuit use the same carried-forward quality cohort.
- The stale V15.2 report title is gone.

WHAT v16.2 DOES NOT CHANGE
- No entry thresholds were loosened.
- No safety checks were bypassed.
- No discovery sources were added just to increase signal count.
- REAL autopilot is not enabled by this update.
- FAST remains excluded from real live autopilot.

AFTER STARTUP
- /status should say v16.2 QUALITY MEASUREMENT.
- /edge should show the carried-forward proof cohort, including eligible FAST ENTRY closes from v16.1.
- /paperreport should show TIER BREAKDOWN.
- Your open paper positions and learning history come from the shared external database and should still be present.

Keep COPY LIVE and real autopilot OFF while proof economics are negative or immature. No trading bot can guarantee profit.
