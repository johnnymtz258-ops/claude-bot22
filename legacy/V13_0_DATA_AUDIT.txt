Key findings
------------
1. CATE was a strategy-universe blind spot, not simply a threshold miss. The exact Solana CATE token was observed hundreds of times but had zero signal rows; its ~$40M–$50M MC was outside the v12 normal actionable universe. v13 adds a separate established STRUCTURE ENTRY lane instead of raising the micro/meme ceiling globally.

2. GG was a chain/actionability blind spot. The Robinhood token had CONFIRMED signal records, but v12's actionable chain was Solana. v13 adds a manual-only cross-chain alert lane. It is explicitly excluded from real autopilot.

3. Cross-chain GG paths support faster management rather than normal meme targets. One early observed GG setup reached roughly +5.6% before falling nearly -9.7% within 60m; a later deep-liquidity breakout reached roughly +18.1%. v13 therefore starts the experimental lane at +5/+12 profit checkpoints with tighter risk supervision.

4. Journal quote estimates cannot be treated as execution truth. The supplied screenshot shows actual Fomo cash outcomes that disagree with quote-derived database P/L on some trades (for example a displayed GG buy/sell sequence was profitable while the old journal estimate was negative). v13 removes ESTIMATED/MIXED closes from recent-realized-P/L/losing-streak sizing inputs and adds /reconcile.

5. Manual profitable trades were previously lost as learning examples. v13 records explicit manual /buy snapshots into the Decision Ledger so future audits can compare user-spotted chart structures with bot entries and blocked decisions.

6. The evidence still does not justify broadly lowering the normal Solana $50K liquidity floor or deleting recent-crash/realtime/tape safeguards. New opportunity types are added as separate lanes with their own constraints.
