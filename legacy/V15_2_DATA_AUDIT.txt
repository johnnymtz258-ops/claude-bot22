A still-higher >=75 cutoff did not generalize to v15.0 (net negative), while lower cutoffs admitted substantially more loss-heavy paths. v15.2 therefore uses 70 as a DEFAULT FORWARD-PROOF ELIGIBILITY FLOOR, not as a claim of historical profitability.

DESIGN FAILURE FIXED
--------------------
In v15.1, all core shadows could enter proof regardless of score, and the paper loss circuit also included deliberately broad research shadows. This mixed two different purposes:
1) broad data collection, and
2) authorization of live risk.

That architecture could quarantine/pause live guidance because of experiments that were never intended to be live candidates.

V15.2 RESPONSE
--------------
- Keep broad shadow research.
- Mark only high-conviction core shadows proof_eligible.
- Require sample count + token diversity + positive net ROI + win/PF quality before ACTIVE.
- Make the live paper-risk circuit use the same proof cohort.
- Start v15.2 proof fresh; no old trades are grandfathered into authorization.

LIMITATION
----------
The >=70 segmentation was chosen with historical v15.0/v15.1 results available. It is calibration, not independent validation. Fresh v15.2 forward results are required before any live authorization. Meme-token behavior remains non-stationary and execution costs can dominate small trades.
