FOMO BOT v10.7 FLOW+PARTIAL

Why this build exists
---------------------
Review of the v10.6 run from roughly 19:08-22:17 UTC found that the stability gate successfully avoided catastrophic losses on the actual ENTRY OPTION alerts, but six of eight entries were low-motion/low-dollar-turnover setups. At the same time, a small number of sub-$50k-liquidity tokens produced strong moves without getting an entry because the normal liquidity floor was absolute.

Changes
-------
1. Dollar-turnover quality gate
   Normal ENTRY OPTION now requires 5-minute dollar volume equal to at least 0.08% of market cap. STRONG ENTRY requires 0.15%. This is aimed at blocking high-swap but economically tiny churn.

2. Strict MICRO ENTRY rescue
   The normal $50k liquidity floor stays intact. A separate MICRO ENTRY can rescue exceptional $25k-$50k liquidity microcaps only when liquidity/MC is healthy, 5m turnover is very high, buyers dominate, swaps are high, the scout move is controlled, and RugCheck + holder verification are complete. MICRO ENTRY remains manual-only; real autopilot will not buy it automatically.

3. Better falling-knife handling for microcaps
   MICRO ENTRY uses a shorter 3-minute raw-price stability window because microcap flow changes quickly. It can tolerate a slightly deeper pullback only after a measurable recovery from the recent low with positive 5m momentum and buyer pressure. Rapid raw-price breaks still block.

4. Partial-sale workflow fixed
   TAKE PARTIAL PROFIT / EXIT alerts now include the exact contract. After actually selling in Fomo, reply directly to that alert with:
     /sellpct 50
     /sellusd 2
   Friendly aliases also work:
     /sold 50%
     /sold $2
   The bot journals the partial sale and continues tracking the remainder. It still does not place the manual sell itself.

5. Export command permissions
   All .command files are packaged executable in this build.

Important
---------
These filters are data-driven risk controls, not a guarantee of profit. Meme coins can gap, rug, or move faster than the scanner.
