SPECIFIC FAILURES FIXED
-----------------------
1. BLUECHIP chase bug
The v13.0 alert was sent even though the latency ledger recorded scout_move=+14.37%. MANUAL CHAIN ENTRY and STRUCTURE ENTRY were missing the scout-chase cap that the normal Solana lane already used. v13.1 enforces ~+4% max scout move for those lanes.

2. v13.0 cross-chain classifier too broad
Examples such as 4, MON, GOOGLc, IF, DRB, VELVET and the BSC weak paths could pass with very little real dollar turnover or with mediocre 1h structure. v13.1 replaces the broad mode with three explicit shapes: low-liquidity flow, deep-liquidity flow and true breakout.

3. Jimothy weak downtrend structure
v13.1 requires much stronger B/S, swaps and turnover for an established STRUCTURE ENTRY when 1h is <= -5%. This blocks the Jimothy snapshot but preserves the cleaner TripleT/CATE-style pattern.

4. Normal Solana downtrend entries too permissive
v13.1 standard entries use roughly -10%..+10% 1h. Between -10% and -5%, the setup must prove a stronger recovery pocket. Deeper washouts remain eligible only through the separate strict REVERSAL ENTRY lane.

5. Small-position profit realization
popape is a useful example of a market-path win that did not translate cleanly into the user's Fomo result. From the bot alert it reached +26.18% within 30m and never dipped below +4.52% during that window, but the user's small real trade ended around flat/slightly negative after execution/friction. v13.1 raises small-position TP1 to an economic threshold and can recommend closing the whole tiny remainder instead of forcing an uneconomic partial.

NEW QUALITY GOVERNOR
--------------------
STRUCTURE ENTRY and MANUAL CHAIN ENTRY now start each v13.1 run in PROBATION. After at least 6 completed 30m paths in the current build, a lane is automatically QUARANTINED if its recent median max upside, target-hit rate or severe-drawdown rate is unacceptable. Quarantine suppresses BUY alerts from that lane while internal scouting/outcome learning continues. /status reports PROBATION / ACTIVE / QUARANTINED.

NO BROAD LIQUIDITY RELAXATION
-----------------------------
The export still contains huge low-liquidity survivors, but many neighboring low-liquidity candidates suffered large drawdowns. v13.1 therefore does NOT lower the normal $50K Solana liquidity floor or broadly weaken MICRO/security gates.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
v13.0's problem in this holdout was not "the scanner saw nothing." It saw many candidates and expanded into more markets, but the new cross-chain lane converted too many mediocre scouts into BUY calls. v13.1 keeps the discovery expansion while substantially raising the conversion standard.

This audit is descriptive, not a guarantee of future profit. Meme/cross-chain markets remain highly non-stationary and execution fees/slippage can dominate small trades.
